# Orbit Labs Time Off and Benefits

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid time off per year, plus public
holidays. Unused days carry over into the next year, up to a maximum of
five days. Requests of more than five consecutive days need approval at least
three weeks ahead.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from paid time off. Employees can take up to ten paid
sick days per year without a doctor's note. From the third consecutive sick
day, a note is required.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 dollars for courses, books,
and conferences. The budget does not roll over. Conference travel is paid from
a separate travel budget, which the team lead must approve.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave can be taken any time within the first year
after the child's birth or adoption.
