# Orbit Labs Security Guidelines

## Reporting incidents

Any suspected security incident, such as a lost laptop or a phishing email that
was clicked, must be reported within one hour. Send the report to
security@orbitlabs.example or message the on-call engineer in the
#security-help channel. Do not try to investigate on your own first.

## Passwords and access

All accounts must use the company password manager and two-factor
authentication. Passwords are never shared in chat. Access to production
systems is granted per project and reviewed every quarter. Contractors receive
access that expires automatically after 90 days.

## Devices

Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five
minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may not be used to access customer
data. Software outside the approved list needs a request to the IT team, who
answer within two business days.
