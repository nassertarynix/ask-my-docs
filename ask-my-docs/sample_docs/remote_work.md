# Orbit Labs Remote Work Policy

Orbit Labs is a fictional company used to demo Ask My Docs.

## Remote days

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. The two in-office days
are Tuesday and Thursday, when team meetings and design reviews take place.
Anyone who wants a different arrangement must get written approval from their
team lead.

## Working from another country

Working from outside the country of employment is allowed for a maximum of
20 days per calendar year. The employee must notify People Operations at least
30 days in advance, because tax and legal rules differ by country.

## Equipment stipend

Every new hire receives a one-time home office stipend of 600 dollars to buy a
desk, chair, or monitor. Receipts must be uploaded to the expense portal within
14 days of purchase. The laptop is provided by the company and remains company
property.
